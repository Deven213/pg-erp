Mobile app wireframes · Phase 1 of 6

PG ERP mobile application

Wireframes for an Indian PG management app with two separate experiences: the Owner app (business, properties, residents, money) and the Resident app (my room, my rent, my complaints). A restricted Super Admin console runs as a third, separate app. This phase sets the architecture and design system and covers authentication, onboarding and the owner dashboard with property switching.

Sample business used throughout: Deven PG Group, owner Deven Patel, 3 properties: Sunrise Boys PG (Vadodara), Royal PG (Ahmedabad), Krishna PG (Surat). 200 beds total, 164 occupied.

Stage 1–2 · Information architecture

Application sitemap

Three apps share one design system and one backend, but never share navigation. The role comes from the authenticated account on the server (Supabase Auth + Row Level Security), never from a switch in the UI. Codes like M6 map to the 15 modules in the specification.

Data hierarchy the UI follows

Owner→PG Business (tenant)→Property / Branch→Building→Floor→Room→Bed→Resident

Every screen below the business level carries a property scope. The global property switcher sets it; "All Properties" means the business-wide view. Lists, finances, reports and expenses all filter by it.

Touch and layout rules

  • Touch targets ≥ 44 × 44. Primary action sits in the bottom third (thumb zone).
  • One primary button per screen; destructive actions always confirm.
  • Max 4 KPI tiles above the fold; detail lives one tap deeper.
  • Lists replace tables. A "mobile table" is a stacked row: title, 2 meta values, status badge, amount right-aligned.

Design decisions to confirm

  • Deposits and Expenses live inside the Finance tab (segmented), not under More.
  • Reservations sit under Residents and in More; also a dashboard quick action.
  • Staff (warden, manager) log in to the Owner app with restricted permissions (see A-13, D-09). The spec names only Owner and Resident roles, so this needs your sign-off.
  • Password login is optional for owners only. Residents use OTP only.

Known limits of this file

  • Property images are drawn illustrations; real photos will come from owner uploads (Supabase Storage).
  • Screens are static wireframes, not a clickable prototype. Each caption states where taps lead.
  • Hindi and Gujarati are shown as type samples; full translated screens are not drawn.
